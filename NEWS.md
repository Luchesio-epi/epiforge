# epiforge 0.1.0

* Initial release
* Core functions: `epi_import()`, `epi_clean()`, `epi_indicators()`, `epi_curve()`, `epi_report()`
* Supports CSV and Excel input (DHIS2-compatible)
* Weekly and monthly report periods
* District, region, and national geographic levels
* Word (.docx) and PDF output formats
